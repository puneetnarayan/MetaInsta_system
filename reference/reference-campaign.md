# 2. Strategy — Reference Output

## Campaign Objective

Generate qualified leads from working professionals in India who want to communicate more confidently in workplace meetings.

## Target Audience

Working professionals aged approximately 25–45 who understand English but hesitate when speaking in meetings, presentations or workplace conversations.

### Audience situation

They may:

- Know the answer but hesitate to speak.
- Mentally translate before responding.
- Search for the right words while speaking.
- Avoid participating in meetings.
- Worry about making mistakes.
- Want to sound clear and professional rather than simply "speak more English."

## Core Customer Problem

> "I know what I want to say, but when the meeting starts I hesitate, search for words and lose confidence."

The advertising should reflect this situation rather than making broad claims about English fluency.

## Desired Transformation

Move the customer from:

**Hesitation → Clear expression → Greater confidence → More participation**

The campaign should focus on practical workplace communication rather than presenting fluency as an abstract goal.

## Positioning

**Speak in Meetings Workshop** is positioned as a practical, focused workshop for working professionals who want to communicate more clearly and confidently during real workplace situations.

## Core Message

> You may already know what you want to say. The challenge is expressing it clearly and confidently when the moment comes.

The workshop provides a structured way to practise the communication skills needed in meetings and workplace conversations.

## Funnel Angle

**Problem awareness → Recognition → Practical solution → Workshop → Lead**

The first interaction should make the viewer recognise their own situation. The ad should then introduce the workshop as a possible next step.

## Creative Testing Plan

### Test 1 — Problem-led

Focus on hesitation, searching for words and staying silent in meetings.

### Test 2 — Outcome-led

Focus on speaking clearly, expressing ideas and participating confidently.

### Test 3 — Question-led

Use questions that make the viewer reflect on their own meeting experience.

# 8. Quality Rules for Future AI Generation

Every future AI-generated campaign should try to follow these rules:

- Use the customer's actual problem rather than generic marketing language.
- Make the first line immediately relevant to the target customer.
- Avoid unsupported guarantees.
- Avoid exaggerated claims such as "guaranteed results" or "become fluent in 4 days."
- Keep the message specific to the product or service.
- Give the audience a clear reason to continue reading.
- Keep one primary idea per ad.
- Make the CTA consistent with the campaign objective.
- Adapt the language to the selected tone.
- Create different angles rather than three versions that merely reword the same advertisement.
- Keep creative concepts practical enough for a real designer or creator to produce.
- Reel scripts should describe both spoken words and what appears on screen.
- The user should always be able to edit the generated content before using it.
